2. **Label text:** The book gives the words but not their order or whether one label or several. I wrote one label per piece, for example `skirt_front size M cut 1 shell colorway not specified place on fold`. Paired pieces end in `R` or `L`, and the interfacing's fabric word is `interfacing`. Label positions are free, so I put them at the grain x and mid-length. The left back's label sits at the mirrored x, which assumes a mirrored piece's label positions are in its own frame.
