## Ambiguities and decisions of mine
1. **Waistband net versus cut size.** Step 8 calls the band a rectangle of 80 × 10 cm, but the draft tool adds allowances outside the net line. I drew the net as 77 × 8 cm (the chapter's `waistband_finished_length` by two band heights). Its cut line is then exactly 80 × 10 cm, and the fold lands 5 cm from each cut edge.
2. **Allowance prose versus table.** The garment summary says "1 cm side, 3 cm hem" and doesn't mention CB. The §7 table sets CB at 1.5 cm, so I used the table. That also makes the 80 cm band length work out.
3. **Darts.** I put each dart in the boundary as a V with width-0 legs. The waist edges then sum to 18.5 cm without extra truing. A dart as internal lines only would leave 22.5 cm at the waist. The V legs carry no allowance, so the dart edges are a guess.
4. **Waist-to-hip curve.** §5 says "smooth" and gives no numbers. I read §13's description as:
   - The curve leaves the hip vertically, with a control point at half of `waist_to_hip`.
   - The other control point sits `ss_suppress`/3 outward from the waist point, at `waist_to_hip`/6 below the waist line.
   The phrase "a sixth of it" is ambiguous and "in from the waist point" could also point toward CF, so this is my reading.
5. **Corners.** CF is a width-0 fold edge. CB and the waist use trim, the side seams use miter, and the hem uses envelope. All four band corners are miter, so they stay square (D395).
6. **Waistband notches.** I placed five on the lower cut edge: the left CB, three quarter points, and the right CB. The left CB sits 3 cm in from the band's left net end, which makes that 3 cm the hook-and-bar extension. I added no internal line for the extension, only a label.
7. **Grainlines.**
   - Body pieces run from the waist to the hem at x = `quarter_hip`/2. On the front and back this line starts inside the dart's open V.
   - The waistband grain sits at a quarter of the cut width.
   - The tool required a grainline on the interfacing, which the book doesn't specify, so I added one along its length.
8. **Labels.** Each piece has one label with name, size, quantity, L/R or "place on fold", and fabric. The book wants a colorway but gives no value, so the labels say "colorway: not specified". The band's label doesn't say "place on fold" and the front's does.
9. **What the tools can't express.** The piece fields have no material or layer, and there's no sewing graph, zipper or hook-and-bar object. The book says those wait for G3. The back-left piece is the mirror of the back-right.
